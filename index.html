Why Chime?

Engineered specifically to solve the opacity of Indian credit card reward calculations.

🔒

100% Local & Private

Password-protected bank statements are decrypted in-memory. Transactions are stored in a local SQLite database that never leaves your machine.

🧠

Two-Tier Classification

Instant deterministic keyword rules cover major Indian merchants (Swiggy, Zomato, Amazon, utilities), backed by an offline semantic NLP embedding model.

📊

Multi-Month Analytics

Track your cashback realization rate, audit exclusions (fuel, rent, EMI fees), and visualize spend distribution across all your cards over time.

How It Works

Three simple steps to verify your cashback.

1

Drop Your PDF Statement

Select your card, enter your statement password if protected, and upload. Decrypted purely in-memory.

2

Automated Audit & Rules

Transactions are extracted, mapped to reward categories, and checked against monthly caps and exclusions.

3

Review & Analyze

Review the line-by-line reward credit, override any categories if desired, and explore multi-month spend trends.
